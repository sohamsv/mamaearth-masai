### Situation

Following an end-to-end data audit of regional transaction records, Mamaearth achieved a cleaned total revenue of INR 97,358.30 against the raw database revenue of INR 99,860.20. The audit isolated a duplicate reconciliation delta of INR 2,501.90, establishing an accurate baseline for regional performance assessment. Monthly distribution analysis indicates strong customer demand across key periods, highlighted by a true peak month in March (2026-03) generating revenue of INR 20,318.90. Overall sales volumes remain resilient across multiple payment methods, including Card, UPI, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

### Complication

Despite top-line expansion, regional profitability is severely constrained by reverse logistics overhead and channel-specific delivery failures. Return rates diverge dramatically by payment method: Card transactions maintain a low return rate of 14.7% and UPI records 18.9%, whereas the COD return rate spikes to 44.4%. Operational vulnerability is acutely concentrated in regional demographics; the highest risk segment—comprising COD + Tier-2 city orders—exhibits an unsustainable return rate of 54.5%. This high rate of Return to Origin (RTO) creates significant inventory lockup, elevated freight costs, and margin dilution that offset gains achieved during peak sales cycles.

### Resolution

To protect gross margins and streamline fulfillment, regional operations and finance leadership should implement the following targeted measures:

1. **Prepaid Channel Incentivization:** Deploy immediate checkout incentives (e.g., instant wallet cashback or percentage discounts) to transition high-risk buyers from COD to digital rails (UPI/Card), directly reducing baseline exposure.
2. **Tier-2 COD Verification & Friction Gates:** Implement mandatory automated IVR/WhatsApp delivery confirmations and OTP verification for all COD transactions originating from Tier-2 clusters before dispatch to curb the 54.5% segment return rate.
3. **Automated Ledger Reconciliation:** Institutionalize data sanitization protocols within ERP systems to identify duplicate orders dynamically, permanently resolving the 2,501.90 reporting variance.
4. **Ops Performance Thresholds:** Enforce regional delivery SLAs with logistics partners to limit COD transit delays, aiming to depress the aggregate COD return rate from 44.4% down to under 25% over the next two quarters.
